Types of Finance

Personal Finance

Managing individual or household financial activities including budgeting, savings, investments, and retirement planning.

Corporate Finance

Financial activities related to running a corporation, including capital raising, investments, and financial planning.

Public Finance

Government revenue collection and expenditure management including taxation, budgeting, and debt issuance.

Behavioral Finance

Study of psychological influences on investor behavior and their effects on markets.

Investment Finance

Managing assets and securities to maximize returns including stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and ETFs.

International Finance

Financial transactions occurring between countries including foreign exchange, trade, and international investments.

Understanding Loans

Personal Loans

Unsecured loans for personal expenses with fixed interest rates and repayment terms.

Home Loans (Mortgages)

Secured loans for purchasing property with the property itself as collateral.

Auto Loans

Secured loans specifically for vehicle purchases with the vehicle as collateral.

Student Loans

Educational loans designed to cover tuition, books, and living expenses with favorable terms.

Business Loans

Financing for businesses including working capital, equipment purchase, and expansion.

Payday Loans

Short-term, high-interest loans intended to cover expenses until the next paycheck.
